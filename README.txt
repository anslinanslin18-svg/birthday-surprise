HIS BIRTHDAY SURPRISE WEBSITE
==============================

HOW TO VIEW IT
  Just double-click index.html and it opens in your browser.

HOW TO PERSONALIZE IT (open index.html in any text editor, e.g. Notepad,
TextEdit, or VS Code, and search for these near the top of the <script> tag):

  boyfriendName     -> his name
  reasons[]         -> your real 20 reasons you love him
  memories[]        -> captions for your photos (see photos/README.txt)
  places[]           -> your real memorable places + notes
  coupons[]         -> your real cute coupons/vouchers
  letterText        -> your real love letter

HOW TO ADD PHOTOS
  See photos/README.txt inside the photos folder — just drop images in
  with the right filenames and they show up automatically.

HOW TO SEND IT TO HIM
  Option 1: Zip this whole folder and send it — he double-clicks index.html.
  Option 2 (recommended, works on his phone too): upload this folder to a
  free host like Netlify Drop (netlify.com/drop) or GitHub Pages, then just
  send him the link.
