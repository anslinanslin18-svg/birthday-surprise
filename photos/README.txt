Drop your photos in this folder with these exact filenames and they'll
automatically appear on the site (any slot without a matching file just
shows a cute "add a photo" placeholder, so nothing ever looks broken):

MEMORIES PAGE
  memory1.jpg   memory2.jpg   memory3.jpg
  memory4.jpg   memory5.jpg   memory6.jpg

PLACES PAGE
  place1.jpg    place2.jpg    place3.jpg    place4.jpg

Tips:
- Square-ish photos look best on the Memories page.
- JPG or PNG both work — just keep the filenames above exactly.
- Want more or fewer photos? Open index.html, search for "const memories"
  or "const places" near the top of the <script> section, and add/remove
  entries (each one just needs a filename + a short caption).
